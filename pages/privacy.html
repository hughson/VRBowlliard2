Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Who we are

VR Bowlliards 2 is published by Deucejuice Studios ("we", "us"). This policy covers the game on Meta Quest and this website.

What the game collects

We collect the minimum needed to let you play with other people and to keep a record of your best games. Specifically:

  • Your Meta account ID and display name. Provided by the headset through the Meta platform. We use the ID to attribute your scores to you, and the display name so other players can see who they are playing.
  • Game results. Your highest game score, highest frame score, highest run, most balls potted on a break, and the number of games you have played.
  • Live match state, while you are in a multiplayer room. Shot data, ball positions, and the position of your head and hands so your opponent can see your avatar and cue. This is exchanged between players in real time and is not stored by us after the match ends.
  • Voice, if you speak in a multiplayer room. Voice is carried live between players in the room. We do not record or store it.

What we do not collect

  • We do not use any analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service.
  • We do not collect your email address, real name, location, or payment details. Purchases are handled entirely by Meta and we never see your payment information.
  • We do not record video, camera images, or room-scanning data from your headset.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or sell data to anyone.

Who else is involved

  • Meta. Supplies your account ID and display name, and handles store purchases. Meta's own privacy policy applies to their services.
  • Photon (Exit Games GmbH). Carries live multiplayer traffic and voice between players. Match traffic passes through their servers so that two headsets can talk to each other.
  • Google Firebase. Stores the game results listed above.

These providers process data on our behalf so the game can function. We do not share your data with anyone else.

Children

VR Bowlliards 2 is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Meta Quest headsets have their own age requirements.

How long we keep it

Game results are kept until you ask us to remove them. Live match and voice data is not retained once the match is over.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your stored game results at any time, or ask for a copy of what we hold. Email support@deucejuice.com from the account you play on, or tell us your in-game display name, and we will action it. Playing entirely offline in single player sends nothing to our servers.

This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and does not track visitors. It loads a web font from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
