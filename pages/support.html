Requirements

  • Meta Quest 2, Quest Pro, Quest 3 or Quest 3S
  • Roughly 2 × 2 m of clear play space for full-stroke play; seated and smaller spaces are supported with a shortened stroke
  • An internet connection for multiplayer

Common questions

My real cue is out of alignment

Open the menu, turn on Real cue, and choose Align Real Cue. Your room appears around you and the game walks you through it step by step: it measures the cue as you move it, then draws a see-through cue over your real one so you can check the two agree before you keep it. Run it again whenever the controller has moved on the cue.

I can't reach the shot

Two controls help, both listed on the menu's Controls page. Adjust bridge changes how far the cue extends past your front hand. Orbit the cue ball walks your view around the table without moving in your room, and Orbit distance sets how far out it sits. Between them, full shots work in a small space.

The table is the wrong height for me

Adjust height raises and lowers the table to suit your standing height, or to let you play seated.

I can't see my friend's room

Private rooms aren't listed. You need the room code from whoever created it. Public rooms appear in the list automatically.

I want to see that shot again

Replay last shot plays the shot back.

I'm left-handed

Left-handed in the menu swaps the bridge and stroke hands.

The table and scores are hard to see

Low vision in the menu makes the table, the balls and the scores easier to pick out.

Where are the leaderboards?

On the pub wall behind the poker table, and on this site out to the top 100.

Report a bug

Post it in the Discord. Include your headset model, the version number shown in the menu, and what you were doing when it happened. For anything about how a shot behaved, a short clip is worth far more than a description.
