Requirements

  • Meta Quest 3 or Quest 3S
  • Roughly 2 × 2 m of clear play space for full-stroke play; seated and smaller spaces are supported with a shortened stroke
  • An internet connection for multiplayer

Common questions

My shots feel too strong or too weak

Shot power maps your stroke speed to cue speed. Adjust it in Settings → Physics → Shot Power. Nothing about your swing needs to change — the curve rescales around it.

The tracked cue drifts out of alignment

Re-run the cue calibration from Settings → Real Cue → Calibrate, holding the cue level with the tip on the table's centre spot.

I can't see my friend's room

Only public rooms are listed. Private rooms require the room code from the host.

Report a bug

Include your headset model, the game version from the title screen, and what you were doing when it happened. Physics reports are much more useful with a short clip.
