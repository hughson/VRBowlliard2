Requirements

  • Meta Quest 3 or Quest 3S
  • Roughly 2 × 2 m of clear play space for full-stroke play; seated and smaller spaces are supported with a shortened stroke
  • An internet connection for multiplayer

Common questions

My real cue is out of alignment

Open Settings, turn on Real Cue, and press Align. Passthrough switches on and a ghost cue appears in front of you — line your actual cue up with it and press A (or X if you're left-handed). That single capture is all the calibration needs.

I can't reach the shot

Two controls help, both on the Controls tab. Adjust Bridge (hold the bridge grip and slide) changes how far the cue extends past your front hand. Orbit Cue Ball walks your view around the table without moving in your room, and Orbit Distance sets how far out it sits. Between them, full shots work in a small space.

The table is the wrong height for me

Adjust Height on the Controls tab raises and lowers the table to suit your standing height, or to let you play seated.

I can't see my friend's room

Private rooms aren't listed. You need the room code from whoever created it. Public rooms appear in the list automatically.

I want to see that shot again

Replay Last Shot on the Controls tab plays the shot back.

I'm left-handed

Left-Handed Mode in Settings swaps the bridge and stroke hands, and the alignment capture button moves from A to X.

Report a bug

Post it in the Discord. Include your headset model, the version number shown at the bottom of the settings panel, and what you were doing when it happened. For anything about how a shot behaved, a short clip is worth far more than a description.
