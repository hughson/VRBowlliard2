About VR Bowlliards 2

A Deucejuice Studios game for Meta Quest 3.

Bowlliards is bowling played on a pool table. Ten frames, two shots a frame, ten object balls on the cloth. Run all ten in one visit and you've struck; clear them in two and you've spared. The scoring is bowling's — strikes and spares carry into the next frame — but every point comes off a cue ball.

Real cue physics

Shots are resolved with a physically-based cue strike model rather than a simple impulse: tip offset drives english and follow/draw, cue elevation produces jumps and massé, and sidespin deflects the cue ball off the aim line the way a real tip does. Cushions, throw, and ball-to-ball collisions are modelled rather than approximated.

Play together

Play head to head with a friend in a private room, or drop into a public hall where rooms hold two tables and you can take one alongside everyone else. Voice chat is carried between players in the same room.

Hand tracking or controllers

Play with controllers, or with a tracked physical cue for a stroke that matches the one you'd make at a table. Passthrough mixed-reality mode puts the table in your own room.
