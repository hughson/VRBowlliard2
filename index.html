The game

🎳

Bowling, on cloth

Ten object balls, two visits a frame. Run the rack in one and you've struck; clear it in two and you've spared. Bowling's scoring, every point off a cue ball.

🎱

Real cue physics

Where you place the tip drives english and draw, elevation gives you jumps and massé, and sidespin deflects the cue ball the way a real tip does.

🤝

Play together

Head to head in a private room with a friend, or drop into a public hall and take a table alongside everyone else.

🕹️

Built for the room you have

Full-stroke play in about two metres of space, with seated and smaller-space play supported by a shortened stroke.
