What's in the pub

🎱

Pool, played properly

Eight ball, nine ball, cowboy pool and free play. Where you place the tip gives you english, draw and follow, and lifting the butt gives you jumps and massé.

🎳

Bowlliards

Ten balls, two visits a frame, bowling's scoring. Play it solo, head to head or as a pair. Bowl a perfect 300. That's all we're saying.

🎯

Darts

501, 301, Cricket and Around the World on three boards, on your own or in a match.

🃏

Poker

Texas Hold'em at a six-seat table, played with your hands: throw your cards in to fold, tap the felt to check. Friends or the house regulars fill the seats.

🎷

Blues on the jukebox

A jukebox of original blues, with a few seasonal records when the calendar calls for them.

🤝

A room full of people

Walk into a public room, or open a private one and share the code. Voice chat at the tables, and the leaderboards on the wall.
